    Pipeline

    How CareerCrawler Works

    Six steps from a bare domain to structured job data.

    1. 1

      Company Website

      Start from any company domain, one or thousands.

    2. 2

      Career Discovery

      Finds the careers page from links, sitemaps and common paths.

    3. 3

      ATS Detection

      Fingerprints the hiring platform behind the page.

    4. 4

      Universal Crawler

      Runs the right adapter, with a generic fallback for custom sites.

    5. 5

      Job Normalization

      Title, location, department and URL in one consistent schema.

    6. 6

      Results

      Deduplicated jobs, ready for dashboards, exports and alerts.
